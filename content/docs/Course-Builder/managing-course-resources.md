Resources can be any type of digital content that you want to store and share with users on your platform.

To access Resources, navigate to "<a href="https://app.compendium360.com/users/sign_in?next_path=/resources" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><strong>My Resource Library</strong></a>" from your Dashboard.

![Resource Library](/uploads/screenshot-2023-12-28-at-7-49-00-am.png)

If you have an Employee Handbook, How-To Videos, or PowerPoint Documents, you can upload these to your Resource Library.

You can manage who has access to resources by limiting the resource to specific Teams, Roles, and Sub Accounts.

![Resource Settings](/uploads/screenshot-2023-12-28-at-7-51-53-am.png)

For example, you may have an Employee Handbook that is available to ALL Teams and Roles. However, you may have Manager-Specific documents that you only want your Managers to have access to. You are able to determine the level of access to each resource independently.
