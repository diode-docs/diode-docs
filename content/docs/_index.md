Compendium360 combines a powerful candidate tracking tools with an easy to use learning management system (LMS) to make it simple for any business or organization to develop their people.

#### Platform Features

###### Course Builder (Authoring Tool)

Create courses easily with our intuitive course builder or import SCORM courses that you’ve already developed. Build courses with video, text, embedded forms, dynamic forms, quizzes, exams, and more.

![Course Builder](/uploads/screenshot-2025-06-27-at-9-52-09-am.png)

###### Applicant Tracking System (ATS)

Manage applicants all in one place and invite them to your team, course, or program. Accept applications, communicate seamlessly with automations and templates, and coordinate with your admission or hiring team all from one place.

![ATS](/uploads/screenshot-2025-06-27-at-9-53-32-am.png)

###### Templates and Automations

Streamline redundant tasks with templates, sequences, and automations. Whether you want to automatically send "Welcome" emails, enroll learners in a course or learning pathway, or send out reminders, you can create any number of automated sequences and never let an event fall through the cracks.

![Templates](/uploads/screenshot-2025-06-27-at-9-55-13-am.png)

###### E-Commerce (Monetization)

Monetize your content and accept payments from your learners directly to your account. Embed your branded catalog on your marketing website and make your learner's journey seamless.

![](/uploads/screenshot-2025-06-27-at-9-56-30-am.png)
