Welcome to Compendium<sup>360</sup>. We're glad you're here! We'll take a look around the Platform and get you acquainted with the layout and some powerful features available.

#### Dashboard

From your DASHBOARD, you will have a quick view of all your Teams, Tasks, Applicants, Enrollments, and more. When you first create your account, you will be invited to "Create a Team", "Browse Catalog" and more to get started.

#### ![Dashboard](/uploads/screenshot-2023-12-28-at-11-02-53-am.png)Managing Account

You can manage BILLING, ORGANIZATION, <a href="https://support.compendium360.com/article/8l21fd5jl2-how-to-set-up-custom-branding" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><strong>BRANDING</strong></a>, ROLES, CUSTOM DOMAIN, [**CUSTOM LABELS**](https://support.compendium360.com/article/nxcco503yi-how-to-set-up-custom-labels), and eCOMMERCE from the "<a href="https://app.compendium360.com/users/sign_in?next_path=/navigations/manage_account" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><strong>Manage Account</strong></a>" option in your Profile Avatar at the Top Right of your account.

#### ![Manage Account](/uploads/screenshot-2023-12-28-at-11-05-27-am.png)My Training Center

"My Training Center" is where you will find all courses that you are personally enrolled in as well as the Account's Course Catalog. As an Admin in the Course Catalog, you can review Catalog listings, enroll users in a Course, Live Session, or Team, and self-enroll in any of the Catalog listings.

![Training Center](/uploads/screenshot-2023-12-28-at-11-05-27-am-1.png)

#### My Resource Library

The Resource Library is the central repository for all of the digital files that you want users to be able to access. Learn more about creating and managing Resources HERE.

![Resource Library](/uploads/screenshot-2023-12-28-at-11-14-55-am.png)

#### My Tasks

The "Tasks" page is where you can see and manage due dates, whether assigned to you or others. You can assign a Tasks for someone else to complete and engage in messaging with the assignee to check on progress, share files, and much more.

![My Tasks](/uploads/screenshot-2023-12-28-at-11-17-40-am.png)

#### Teams

Your "TEAMS" page is where you can view, edit, and create any number of Teams on your account.

![Teams](/uploads/screenshot-2023-12-28-at-11-23-42-am.png)

#### User Invitations

Once a TEAM is created, you can invite Members to join your Team or Teams.

![Invitations](/uploads/screenshot-2023-12-28-at-11-24-59-am.png)

#### Team Members

Members of one team do not overlap, interact, or have access to members of another team unless a member is assigned to both teams. From TEAMS, you can view a list of Members on a given Team, their start dates, contact information, and more.

![Team Members](/uploads/screenshot-2023-12-28-at-11-25-58-am.png)

#### User Profiles

By clicking into a specific Team Member Profile, you can view, assign, and manage their Profile Information, Courses, Evaluations, Tasks, Communications, Sequences, Resources, and more.

![User Profiles](/uploads/screenshot-2023-12-28-at-11-26-24-am.png)

#### Courses

This is where you CREATE, EDIT, and MANAGE COURSES for your learners. Native-built courses offer very detailed and nuanced capabilities in how a course is presented and graded.

![Courses](/uploads/screenshot-2023-12-28-at-11-33-28-am.png)

Courses currently support Text Lessons, Audio/Text Lessons, Video/Text Lessons, Dynamic Forms, Downloadable Content, Embeddable PDFs, and Quizzes/Exams.

![Course Options](/uploads/screenshot-2023-12-28-at-11-34-17-am.png)

#### Admissions / Recruiting

ADMISSIONS (Recruiting) can be used for Academic Admissions, Commercial Job Recruitment, or Program Vetting, depending on your Organization's needs. Post openings for Programs or Jobs and create custom-built Application Templates for each opening.

![Course Admissions](/uploads/screenshot-2023-12-28-at-11-39-00-am.png)

From here, you can view candidate profiles, communicate, vet and manage candidates all in one place.

![Applicant Status](/uploads/screenshot-2023-12-28-at-11-40-08-am.png)

#### Templates

Templates is where the magic happens. Using Templates, you can really simplify life, automate workflows, generate canned messaging, and much more. You can create Templated Forms, Applications, Messages, and Sequences (Work Flows).

![Template Manager](/uploads/screenshot-2023-12-28-at-12-36-50-pm.png)

#### Live Sessions

Live Sessions connects your <a href="https://support.compendium360.com/article/Zoom.us" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><strong>Zoom.us</strong></a> account directly to your Compendium<sup>360</sup> Platform allowing you to schedule, manage, and hold Live Sessions, Classes, Meetings, etc directly from your Platform Account without ever having to leave.

![Live Session Manager](/uploads/screenshot-2023-12-28-at-12-40-49-pm.png)
