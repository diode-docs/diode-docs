With your zoom account connected, you can create, manage, and host zoom meetings (Live Sessions) directly from your Compendium<sup>360</sup> account. All sessions created and edited on your Compendium<sup>360</sup> account will automatically be synced to your zoom account.

Connecting your zoom account takes less than 1 minute.

**Step I:** Navigate to Live Sessions

Click on "Live Sessions" in the left-hand menu and then click "New Live Session"

![Live Sessions](/uploads/screenshot-2024-01-17-at-11-44-55-am.png)

**Step 2:** Click "Connect Account"

![Connect Account](/uploads/screenshot-2024-01-17-at-11-48-24-am.png)

**Step 3:** Sign In and Authorize Zoom Connection

Be sure that you are signed into the zoom account you want to connect. Once signed into the correct account, authorize the connection by clicking "Allow".

![Authorize Zoom](/uploads/screenshot-2024-01-17-at-11-51-01-am.png)

That's it! You're all set and can now start creating Live Sessions directly in your Compendium<sup>360</sup> Platform.
