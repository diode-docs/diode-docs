The Compendium<sup>360</sup> Platform allows you to enter your own Custom Labels for a variety of fields on your Account. Custom Labels allow you to really tailor the terms, verbiage, and style of your account to the specific vernacular your organization uses.

<a href="https://app.compendium360.com/users/sign_in?next_path=/account_settings?section=custom_labels" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><strong>CLICK HERE</strong></a> to go directly to your Custom Branding page.

**Step 1:** Navigate to your "user avatar" at the top-right of your account and click "<a href="https://app.compendium360.com/users/sign_in?next_path=/navigations/manage_account" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Manage Account</a>" from the drop-down list.

![Manage Account Menu](/uploads/screenshot-2023-12-28-at-7-56-07-am.png)

**Step 2:** Select the [**Custom Labels**](https://app.compendium360.com/users/sign_in?next_path=/account_settings?section=custom_labels) tab

![Custom Labels Menu](/uploads/screenshot-2023-12-28-at-8-44-50-am.png)

**Step 3:** Enter the Custom Labels you want your platform to have and click "Save" once finished.

![Custom Labels Options](/uploads/screenshot-2023-12-28-at-8-46-38-am.png)

Congratulations! You are really providing a high-level touch for your users' experience by enabling custom labels on your account.
