We have a robust Applicant Tracking System (ATS) custom built and native to the Compendium<sup>360</sup> Platform. When a candidate applies to your Program, you can review their application, communicate internally with your admissions team, communicate externally with the applicant, hold online interviews, accept documentation, and enroll them into your Program all from the ATS on your Account.

Clients use the ATS for both education (Admissions) and job placement (Recruitment). You can customize your ATS with custom labels (ie: Job Opening vs Program Opening), custom applications, and custom onboarding/vetting workflows to meet the various demands within your organization.
