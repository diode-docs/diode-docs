Absolutely! You can have an unlimited number of accounts on Compendium<sup>360</sup>. Accounts can be individual "Parent" accounts or your can create a Parent-Child Account structure with a single Parent Account and as many Child (Sub) Accounts as you need.

Typically, Sub Accounts are used by organizations who have their own clients and want to provide a custom, high-touch, branded experience that is specific to each of their customers. Most content creation can happen at the Parent Account and be shared to all or selected Sub Accounts, as needed.

Sub Accounts can also be used in other ways such as to provide an organization (Business, Organization, School, Church, Restaurant Chain, etc) the ability to provide custom-branded experiences for each location they serve.
