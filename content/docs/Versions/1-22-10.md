Diode Collab v1.22.10 was released on September 4th, 2026.

* Features
  * Show Zone owner name on invitations
* Bug fixes:
  * Windows disk space detector

## Show Zone owner name on invitations

Invites sent from a Zone Admin or Owner now land with the Owner's username as the inviting entity.  In the past they would land with the username of whatever Zone peer (any role) happened to initiated the invitation connection.  This will make it easier to identify safe Zones to accept invitations from.

## Fix: Windows disk space detector

Windows systems could report incorrect disk space - this has been fixed and Drive settings now proactively shows the available space on the selected disk.
