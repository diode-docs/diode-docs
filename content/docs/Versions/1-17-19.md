Diode Collab v1.17.19 was released on August 27, 2025 to fix an edge case with publishing Zone Discover page cards

* Bug Fixes
  * Make publishing Zone Discover cards more reliable

## Fix: Make publishing Zone Discover cards more reliable

Updates to Zone Discover card information would sometimes not be published, creating a situation in which subsequent updates would also fail.  This process is now more robust.
