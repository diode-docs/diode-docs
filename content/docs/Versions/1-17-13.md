Diode Collab v1.17.13 was released on July 29, 2025

* Bug Fixes
  * Limit push notification broadcast

## Fix: Limit push notification broadcast

In certain situations, push notifications could be sent out to newly added team members even before the new member accepted the invitation to the Zone.  This could happen if the message sender shared another Zone with the message recipient.

This has been fixed - now push notifications are only sent if the receiving member has actively joined the Zone.
