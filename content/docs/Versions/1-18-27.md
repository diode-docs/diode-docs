Diode Collab v1.18.27 was released on March 11, 2026.

* Features
  * Better profile switching for mobile
  * Improve tunneled browser
  * Allow Nomad plans to selectively publish to Discover page

## Better profile switching

When switching between profiles, mobile now prompts the user to relaunch the app. This makes it faster / more intuitive to switch between profiles.

## Improved tunneled browser

The performance of the built-in tunneled browser has been significantly improved for both desktop and mobile.

## Allow Nomad plans to selectively publish to the Discover page

All plans can now select when they are published to the Discover page. In the past, Nomad plans (free plans) "force published" a Zone's Discover Card to the public Discover page when it had more than 3 team members.  This approach was modeled after Github: free means share with others.  However, after talking to our users, we realized that, although the Zone owner still had full control of who actually joined the Zone, the required public facing Discover Card felt antithetic to Diode's privacy commitment.  So, we have retracted this requirement.
