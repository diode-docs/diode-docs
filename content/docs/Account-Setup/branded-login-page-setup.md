###### **(Video) Single Custom Branded Login Page for all Connected Accounts**

Users across all of of your connected accounts can log in via your Parent (Master) Account's branded login page.

![Branded Login Page](/uploads/1-login-page.png)

*In the example above, ACME Training Center is the Parent Account and it has 4 sub accounts. Any user from any of the 5 accounts can use the ACME Training Center login page and they will be directed to their designated account.*

This removes the necessity of providing each of your sub accounts a different login page. You can now have a single “Client Login” button on your website and any user from any of your accounts can access their custom account from this one login page.

If you still prefer to host a separate login page for each sub account, you can still do this.
