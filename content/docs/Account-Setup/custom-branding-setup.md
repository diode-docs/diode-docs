Custom Branding is a great way to treat your users to an immersive experience. You can set your own logo, primary and secondary colors.

<a href="https://app.compendium360.com/users/sign_in?next_path=/account_settings?section=branding" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><strong>CLICK HERE</strong></a> to go directly to your Custom Branding page.

**Step 1:** Navigate to your "user avatar" at the top-right of your account and click "<a href="https://app.compendium360.com/users/sign_in?next_path=/navigations/manage_account" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Manage Account</a>" from the drop-down list.

![Manage Account Menu](/uploads/screenshot-2023-12-28-at-7-56-07-am.png)

**Step 2:** Select the "<a href="https://app.compendium360.com/users/sign_in?next_path=/account_settings?section=branding" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Branding</a>" tab

![Branding Menu](/uploads/screenshot-2023-12-28-at-7-59-45-am.png)

**Step 3:** Select your Primary color, Secondary Color, Logo, and then click "Save"

![Branding Options](/uploads/screenshot-2023-12-28-at-8-25-06-am.png)

Congratulations! You just set up an immersive environment for your users that really showcases your organization.
