Absolutely! Compendium<sup>360</sup> provides eCommerce capabilities that allow users to charge for Course enrollments, Live Sessions, and Team enrollments.

#### **Scalability**

The Platform is infinitely scalable and there is no limit to the number of users, enrollments, or connected accounts you can have.

#### **Parent-Child (Sub) Accounts**

From the Parent account, you can host and charge for enrollments across all of your Sub Accounts while keeping it simple by connecting a single Stripe account for all Parent and Sub Account transactions.

#### **Make a Single Course and Offer it in a Paid Version and a Free Version- at the Same Time.**

A very powerful feature that our clients love is the ability to have a commercially-available course available for enrollment to one group of users for a cost and make that same course available for FREE to a different set of users at the same time. This mitigates the need to send users coupon codes for free enrollments. It also removes the need to have two duplicate courses (1 paid version and 1 free version) where any changes or modifications to the course would need to be done twice. One course; one single location for edits; paid and free options available to those who YOU select.

#### **Charge for Team Enrollments**

Team enrollments can be free or paid. Teams are basically 'groups' of people on your platform. If you are an education institution (ie: college), you'll re-label your 'Teams' as 'Classes'. Classes can have an enrollment fee associated with them. Once participants are in the 'Class', you can choose to enroll them in courses, learning pathways, live sessions, and more.

#### **Charge of Live Sessions**

Live sessions are where your people can meet in real-time on a video call. You can generate, schedule, manage, and hold live sessions all from within your Account. These sessions can be free or paid to enroll and it's all up to you.
