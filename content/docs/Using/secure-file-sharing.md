Many people use Diode Collab to securely share files because everything is end-to-end encrypted, never stored on a server, easy to share, and accessible only to people in their Zone.

## Sending Files in Chat from Zone

Share any file within your Zone to a channel or DM by selecting the file and clicking on the "chat" icon in the upper righthand corner.

![](/uploads/select-file-and-share.png)

![](/uploads/select-chat-to-send-1.png)

## Sending Files in Chat from Device

Share any file from your device to a channel or DM by selecting the file and clicking on the "paperclip" icon in the lefthand corner of your chat box.

![](/uploads/send-file-from-device.png)

## Manage File Access with Private Drives

You can narrow down who in your Zone you want to see which files by creating [private drives](https://app.docs.diode.io/docs/private-drives/), only allowing certain members to access certain areas of the Zone.

![](/uploads/private-drives-example.png)

## External Sharing

If someone you want to share a file with doesn’t have Diode Collab, you can easily create a [secure share link](https://app.docs.diode.io/docs/using/share-a-file-or-folder-via-web-browser/) that opens directly in their browser by selecting the desired file and clicking the paperclip icon in the righthand corner.

![](/uploads/secure-share-link.png)

| [Plan](https://app.docs.diode.io/docs/features/pricing-and-plans/) | **Description** |

| **Nomad** | Share securely across all your linked devices. Ideal for syncing passwords, notes, and personal information. |
