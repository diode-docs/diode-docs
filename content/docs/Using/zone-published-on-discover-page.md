The "[Discover Page]()" is the list of Zones within Diode that can be found under your username's drop-down.

Your Zone with automatically publish to the Discover Page if you have 3+ users within your Zone. To un-publish the Zone, you either have to get your Zone members under 3 users or update your subscription to a [paid plan](https://diode.io/pricing/).

### **How To Un-publish**

Navigate to settings within your Zone and scroll to the "Publicly Accessible Information" section. From there, select "Zone Discovery."

![](/uploads/public-accessbility-1.png)

Select "un-publish" in the upper righthand corner.

![](/uploads/unpublish-zone.png)
